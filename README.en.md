# ÉTS+

[Français](README.md)

Your ÉTS schedule, grades, mail and transit, in one Android app.

No account to create, no server between you and the school: sign in with your ÉTS account and that's it.

Made by [Studio Synocti](https://synocti.ca). Not an official ÉTS app.

## Build

```
./gradlew assembleGithubRelease
```
